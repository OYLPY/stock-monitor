This is a local Worker schedule; the Worker process must be running to enqueue new slots.
- `src/features/reports/task.ts` uses the existing research workflow and archive. DAILY_AUDIT also runs the deterministic, read-only paper-ledger audit and includes its status and drawdown evidence in the archive. Missing research evidence continues through the existing `NOT_VERIFIED`/zero-candidate path.

The archived report's `generatedAtMs` remains the report `asOf` time; unavailable timestamps remain unknown.
