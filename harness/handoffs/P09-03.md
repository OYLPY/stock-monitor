## Objective

Implement three deterministic initial long-only strategies with strict parameter boundaries, explicit evidence handling, and signals compatible with the P09-02 next-open backtest engine. Acceptance: [GitHub Issue #60](https://github.com/OYLPY/stock-monitor/issues/60).

## Established facts

- `NormalizedOhlcvSeries` supplies point-in-time completed bars and split adjustments; `runBacktest` consumes `BacktestSignal[]` and decides fills.
- An earnings event and bar with equal availability timestamps have no reliable ordering; the strategy waits for a later bar.
- Fixture prices produce `DEMO_ONLY`; missing earnings coverage, unknown bar time, incomplete adjustment coverage, or late split evidence blocks affected signals and marks the result `NOT_VERIFIED`.
- No market data provider or paid service was added; the approved standard free GitHub Actions runner is running Quality CI.
